The Notice Board

The Yawning Portal · Castle Ward

Nailed, pinned and wax-sealed. Tear off the tag to claim a job. First tag taken, first served — the Watch does not adjudicate disputes over parchment.

The board is bare this tenday. Whatever was nailed here has been taken, torn down, or settled the hard way — come back when the ink is wet.

Board Rules

Postings are cleared at each tenday’s turn. The house keeps no record of who took what, and remembers nothing if asked.

  1. One tag per hand.
  2. Blood spilled over a posting is blood spilled in the Portal, and Durnan settles that himself.
  3. Patrons who do not pay are not posted twice.
